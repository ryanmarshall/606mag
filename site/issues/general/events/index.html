06.04.04 - the sixosix open house
We're looking for you! Yes you, the really creative person who stayed here in Chicago while the rest of your wussy cohorts left to New York because they claimed there was no work or scene here. Well now you have your chance to rub it in their face that the'y're living in a shoebox in the Village, while you're helping to start a publication here in chicago living like a king in your roomy Logan Square greystone.

sixosix is looking for photographers, designers/illustrators and writers to work with us, so come to the open house, meet us and show us what you've got.

1259 N. Milwaukee Ave. #2
4:30 pm- 8 pm

Collaboraction and sixosix present PROM, the newest experiential Total Environment.

The Logan Square Auditorium will be transformed into an ethereal plane of intrigue and seduction.

20 Angels will beguile and enchant Prom goers in a heavenly chamber: they will guide you through the lush 7 Minutes of Heaven make-out room, scold you in the Fallen Angel Detention Center and lure you into dares as part of the Dare Rites- which will help determine the Prom King and Queen!
