You can do it punk rock. You can do it disco. You can do it by yourself, hovering over a table lined with good luck charms, a Styrofoam cup of coffee, and a wide selection of ink blotters, or you can do it in a group, toasting free pink shots on the call of “O-69.” Catholic school cafeteria, vintage speakeasy, rainbow nightclub—you name it, you can play it. It's Bingo, and it's featured around Chicago nearly every night of the week.
