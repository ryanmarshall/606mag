I met this girl while I was bartending a party one night. Her third sentence to me was “Are we going to your place?” Now, to most guys this sounds like they just hit the jackpot, but to me, for some reason, I saw a huge red flag. Did I heed this red flag? No!

This girl had what seemed to be an accent. It's not an accent you have ever heard before. This girl had made up her own accent. She would roll her R's in every word. It's not even Latino-sounding. She would even add rolled R's to words that had no R's. Sounds impossible, right? I thought so too but not for her! I guess that's what people from L.A. do with their spare time. They make up accents.
