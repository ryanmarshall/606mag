Ahh …Chicago, the first “ Modern City ” of architecture. We are the big leagues. We start architectural movements here, and people from all around the globe come just to ogle at the glass and cement colossusus and the Prairie-style structures that dot our urban landscape. If you want to learn about the famous architecture of Chicago , take one of the many fine tours or pick up one of the countless books on the subject. But what about those overlooked buildings? What about the buildings that you walk by and rather than inspiring awe, they make you uneasy or confused? What about the buildings that the tours won't take you to? Don't worry, because we at sixosix are happy to take it upon ourselves to fill you in on the buildings that make us go “huh”?
