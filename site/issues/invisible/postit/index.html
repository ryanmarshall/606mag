I walk with my head up and my ears open. moving around the city, wherever i may be going, i bring my camera. when i hear a snippet of a conversation, i stop. i remove a pad of post-it notes and a marker from my purse, and write the fragment as i hear it. after sticking the post-it note on a convenient object of my choice, i take a picture of the note. the note remains; i continue
