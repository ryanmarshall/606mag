issue eleven out now, in this issue:

1. talk derby to me
What you don't know about roller derby could hurt you. The Windy City Rollers are poised and ready to take Chicago by storm with tight shirts, fishnet stockings and roller skates. Editor-in-Chief Nicholas Ziegler went undercover at their recent fundraiser. He almost didn't come out alive.

2. passage
You walked through, or at least past, an alley on the way to work this morning. What your alley didn't have was a fashion shoot. sixosix Fashion Editor Stephanie McNiel shows you what it's like when couture and your back alley collide..

3. giving thanks with family-fun arts and crafts
Fall is the time to gather 'round the dinner table and give thanks with your family friends. sixosix looks at how you should be celebrating. Get ready to break out your crayons and construction paper, as Art Directors Christine Mladic and Sarah Ferone show you the true meaning of Thanksgiving.

4. threads
You know your favorite pair of jeans? Your default, trusted pair that will get you by, that keep you comfortable? Yeah, those. They won't last forever, so contributing writer Kami Bremyer tells you just what to do about it.
