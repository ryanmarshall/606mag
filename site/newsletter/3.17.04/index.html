Issue four of 606 Magazine hits April 1. A short preview:

30 under 30 / The New Chicago: There are 2,896,016 people living in the city of Chicago. Thirty of them made our list. They’re the ones who will make all the differences in the years to come. Ladies and gentlemen, the movers and shakers in the city: 30 notables under 30 years old.

Pursuit of Happiness: Remember that time you put the baseball card in the spokes of your bike? That made you happy. And you’ve been searching for something that pure ever since. You’re in luck: Sarah Buishas explores the ins and outs of happiness, the pursuit of it and, of course, how we get there.
