How to Succeed in Business Without Really Trying       

Ever see How to Succeed in Business Without Really Trying ? It's a mediocre movie from the ‘60s, and is kinda like Office Space , just with elaborate dance numbers . And while it's not as good as Office Space , it is, nonetheless, an excellent send-up of office politics, and a good movie to see before starting any job.

That's not to say this movie is perfect: Plenty of parts are over-the-top goofy, only one song is particularly memorable and the cast calls to mind both the adjectives “good” and “bad.”

Nonetheless, this story of how a window cleaner quickly ascends the corporate ladder (thanks to an eponymous self-help book), is sure to educate anyone entering the working world.
