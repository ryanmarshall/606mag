On May 13, 1917 , a luminous apparition of the Virgin Mary appeared to three peasant children and delivered a prophecy . The children gave predictions of World War II, the spread of communism and a third prophecy, which the children wrote down and gave to the Vatican . In 2000 the Vatican finally revealed the third secret: a description of the 1981 assassination attempt on Pope John Paul II.

In 1777 while encamped in Valley Forge , George Washington had a vision that has been recorded and now sits in the Library of Congress. More than 4,000 words long, the vision contains the future of America, with lines such as, “Son of the Republic, look and learn, I cast my eyes upon America and beheld villages and towns and cities springing up one after another until the whole land from the Atlantic to the Pacific was dotted with them .”

Many people believe these lines written in 1654 by Nostradamus predicted 9/11:

In the City of God there will be a great thunder,
Two brothers torn apart by Chaos, while the fortress endures, the great leader will succumb. The third big war will begin when the big city is burning

Did you know that you can fold the new $5, $10, $50 and $100 bills and they show the World Trade Center attack in sequence? Add in the $20 and you have the complete sequence.

Famous T.V. psychic Miss Cleo was arrested in February 2003 for deceptively misrepresenting in her advertising that a reading would be provided at no cost. Her psychic creadabilty was unchallenged by the FTC and remains intact. Thank God.

The first clear reference to Tarot cards is from a collection of sermons found in the Steele Manuscript. The sermon is thought to date from approximately 1450-1470 and is a diatribe against games of chance. It gives a detailed description of the Tarot trumps , not only numbering them but naming them as well. .

John Edwards is believed to talk to dead relatives of participants on his TV show Crossing Over. Some believe his talent lies in his ability to do cold readings, which is a sleight-of-tongue technique fast-talking artists or so-called readers use. Cold reading has many methods whereby the reader can get out of a blatantly wrong guess with extreme speed. So fast that, unless you listen very carefully or are able to review a transcript of what was actually said during the reading, few would ever notice.

The Romans believed in the practice of augury , in which birds were dissected and analyzed. The future was predicted by the layout, size and condition of the birds' internal organs.




Edgar Cayce gave more than 14,000 prophetic readings between 1901 and 1945 covering more than 10,000 topics. When individuals came to him with a question, he would place himself into an induced sleep state in order to see the future. For many years the information dealt mainly with medical problems. Eventually the scope of his readings expanded to include such topics as meditation, dreams, reincarnation, and prophecy. The transcripts have provided the basis for over 300 popular books about Cayce's work.

The Voynich Manuscript has been dubbed “The Most Mysterious Manuscript in the World.” Wilfrid M. Voynich discovered it in 1912 amongst a collection of ancient manuscripts. It is written in either a code or an unknown language and to this day has been untranslatable. Theories abound that the manuscript contains medical information on eternal life.
