04.01.04 The April issue of sixosix magazine features 30 Chicagoans under the age of 30 who have achieved an unusual level of success for their age within a variety of fields. These are the people that are making Chicago a city we are proud to call home.

“30 Under 30: The New Chicago” includes people from all backgrounds and professions. From entrepreneurs to artists, performers to priests and athletes to scientists, the list displays the remarkable and eclectic talents of the rising stars in Chicago. Each discusses the person, their talents and their accomplishments with an accompanying photo and Web link for more information. sixosix not only commends, but also supports and encourages the people that make Chicago one of the most interesting cities in the world.

Some notable members of the “30 Under 30” list include Alpana Singh, sommelier at Everest; Debra Pickett, columnist for the Sun-Times; Jeffery Brown, graphic novelist; Mark DeNardo, electronic musician; and Amy Schroeder, publisher of Venus Zine.

sixosix magazine covers all that influences, entertains and affects Chicago and its residents, offering features, photo essays, debates and fashion images aimed at the “twenty-thirty somethings” that help to shape and change our diverse and dynamic city.
